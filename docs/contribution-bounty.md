# Contribution bounty plan

**Status: proposed — payment platform not configured.**

This project may use fixed-price bounties for concrete maintenance work. A bounty is **not active** until a GitHub issue records all of the following:

- the exact task and acceptance criteria;
- the fixed amount and currency;
- the funding source and payment platform;
- the payout timing and method; and
- the maintainer responsible for review and acceptance.

Until those details are published in the issue, contributions are voluntary and no payout is promised. A future payment platform may be added later; that change will be recorded in the issue and the evidence ledger.

Payment will never depend on a Star, follow, review, referral, OpenAI application, or any other artificial engagement. Rewards are for accepted engineering work only.

## Proposed first task

The first candidate is a small, reviewable improvement to the GitHub Actions test matrix or fixture coverage. The task should be opened as a separate issue only after the acceptance criteria and funding terms are ready.

## Evidence rule

Record the issue URL, final commit, acceptance decision, and payout confirmation after completion. Do not report a planned bounty as paid work, external adoption, or project usage.
